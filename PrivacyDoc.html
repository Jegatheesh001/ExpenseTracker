Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

Effective Date: November 22, 2026
Application: Expense Tracker (com.jegatheesh.expenseTracker)
Developer: Jegatheesh

🛡️ Privacy Summary: Expense Tracker is an offline-first app with no developer servers, user accounts, ads, or analytics tracking. Your financial records and receipts remain on your device unless you initiate optional AI features, foreign-currency rate lookups, user-shared backups, or Google Assistant queries.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Expense Tracker ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled in the Expense Tracker Android application in accordance with Google Play User Data standards.

2. Data Collection & Processing

  • 2.1 Financial & Profile Records (Local): Expenses, amounts, currencies, dates, categories, tags, billed items, cash/bank balances, budgets, and subscriptions across local profiles are stored strictly on-device in SQLite and SharedPreferences. No developer servers store this data.
  • 2.2 Receipts & Attachments: Receipt photos are saved in the app's internal sandbox directory and leave your device only if you run optional AI Receipt Scanning (OCR).
  • 2.3 Audio & Voice Interaction: Voice recordings in the AI chatbot are cached temporarily, sent only when using Google Gemini (not OpenAI-compatible endpoints), and deleted immediately after transmission. When chat audio playback is unmuted, reply text is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (Gemini) or your configured OpenAI-compatible /audio/speech endpoint.
  • 2.4 Shared Content & Assistant Integrations: Text shared via Android's Share menu or prefilled via Google Assistant App Actions / Android AppFunctions creates a local draft requiring your manual review before saving. Home screen widgets and read-only Assistant/AppFunction queries (balances, budgets, spending summaries, subscriptions, categories) hide or block data while the device is locked; unlocked queries return requested summaries to Google Assistant or the invoking system assistant under their respective policies.
  • 2.5 API Keys & Credentials: User-provided Gemini and OpenAI-compatible API keys are encrypted in Android Keystore (flutter_secure_storage) and never accessible to the developer.
  • 2.6 Local Notifications: Daily reminders and subscription due alerts (displaying title, amount, currency, and due date) are scheduled locally on-device using your device timezone, with no remote push servers.

4. Device Permissions

Only permissions required for active features are requested:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Connects over HTTPS to configured AI/TTS providers and api.frankfurter.dev for exchange rates.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Optional voice input in the AI chatbot.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, & VIBRATE: Optional local daily expense and subscription due reminders (including reboot rescheduling).
  • System Camera & File Pickers: Receipt capture and backup import/export use Android's on-demand system pickers without broad camera or storage manifest permissions.

5. Data Retention & Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

  • In-App Deletion: Open Settings → Danger Zone → Delete All Data and type delete to irreversibly wipe the SQLite database, preferences (including cached AI insights and exchange rates), receipt attachments, and scheduled notifications. API keys in Android Keystore can be cleared individually in AI Settings or removed by uninstalling.
  • Uninstalling & Cloud Backup: Android OS cloud Auto Backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false"). Uninstalling removes all internal app data, preferences, attachments, and Keystore keys.
  • User-Controlled Backups: Exported .txt or .zip backups (which exclude API keys, custom AI URLs, cached insights, and cached exchange rates) are saved or shared via the system Share Sheet strictly to locations you choose.

6. Security

  • Sandbox & Backup Isolation: Protected by the Android app sandbox with cloud Auto Backup disabled.
  • Secure Credential Storage: API keys are encrypted in Android Keystore.
  • Transport Security: All remote AI, TTS, and exchange-rate requests enforce HTTPS/TLS (plain HTTP is blocked for remote hosts and permitted only for local self-hosted addresses: localhost, 127.0.0.1, 10.0.2.2).
  • Lock-Screen Protection: Home screen widget and Assistant/AppFunction queries hide or refuse financial data while the device is locked.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect or maintain personal data from children.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:

  • Access & Portability: Export all your financial records and receipts anytime via Backup Data.
  • Erasure: Permanently delete all records in one tap using Delete All Data.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell or monetize your personal or financial information.
